How this portal calculates cash flow: Recorded days use their actual completed daily sales and, following the current Macleen's accounting rule, Vault Drops are included in sales. Every blank day is automatically filled using the average daily sales from all recorded actual sales days. Projected days are clearly marked below. COGS is automatically fixed at 60% of either actual or projected sales. Additional income and expenses come from the recurring schedules you create.
