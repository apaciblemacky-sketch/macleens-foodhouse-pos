FINANCIAL STATEMENTS & BUNDLE DEALS V9
- Adds an Admin-only Financial Statements workspace with tabbed Income Statement,
  Balance Sheet, Statement of Cash Flows, Debit/Credit Journal, and Source controls.
- Generates double-entry journal lines from completed POS sales, vault drops,
  direct expenses, paid cash-flow expenses, and (when enabled) unpaid scheduled
  cash-flow items. Source records are never changed by reporting exclusions.
- Lets you edit Target Profit Margin % and fallback Cost %. The fallback Cost %
  is used only for sale lines that have no recorded item cost, including vault drops.
- Lets you add/delete balanced daily adjusting entries (one debit and one credit
  for the same amount) without changing original sales or expense history.
- Keeps all completed cashier/POS sales, vault drops, and direct expenses in
  Financial Statements, with no option to hide individual transactions.
- Lets you exclude/include only whole recurring plans from Cash Flow Manager.
  One choice applies to every paid, unpaid, and scheduled occurrence of that
  plan without deleting its original cash-flow history.
- Removes the separate Investing Activities section from the Cash Flow
  statement. Manual cash entries that are neither operating nor financing are
  shown as Other manual cash adjustments instead.
- Adds product Bundle Deals in Admin. Pick active products, choose a percentage
  or fixed-peso discount, then customers can add the calculated bundle to their
  storefront basket. The server recalculates deal prices and deducts stock from
  every product in the bundle, so browser price edits cannot change the deal.
- Fixed bundles intentionally do not accept products with required choices or
  priced sizes yet; sell those individually until a chosen-option bundle flow is
  added. This avoids silently using the wrong flavor, size, or preparation.

DIGITAL ASSETS, GCASH & HELP BOT V10
- Lets Admin upload protected PDF, document, spreadsheet, image, ZIP, HTML,
  and other non-executable digital assets up to 20 MB by default.
- Releases a ready download only after payment is confirmed. Each order has a
  private tracking link plus a unique access code, protected download limits,
  and Admin code reset controls.
- Keeps HTML files as forced downloads instead of running them in the system.
- Adds PayMongo hosted GCash checkout support when PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY is set
  only in Render Environment Variables; Manual GCash/cashier verification stays
  available until the merchant setup is complete.
- Adds a Digital Help Bot with safe built-in/AI answers and a direct Facebook
  support handoff to https://www.facebook.com/macleensdigital/.
