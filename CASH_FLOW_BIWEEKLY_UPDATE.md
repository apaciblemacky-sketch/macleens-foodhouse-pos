# Cash Flow Bi-weekly Update

The 2-Year Cash Flow portal now supports **Bi-weekly (Every 2 Weeks)** schedules for both expenses and additional income.

- Bi-weekly means once every 14 days.
- Duration is the number of occurrences.
- Maximum duration is 52 bi-weekly occurrences, matching the two-year planning horizon.
- Existing Daily, Weekly, and Monthly schedules are unchanged.
