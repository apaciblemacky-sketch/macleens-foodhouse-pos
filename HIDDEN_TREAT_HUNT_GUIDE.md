# Hidden Treat Hunt — Quick Guide

## What it does

A Hidden Treat is a small, account-bound reward that a logged-in loyalty member can discover once. It can live on one product in the storefront, the Loyalty Portal, or Community.

- **Loyalty points:** credits points immediately.
- **Voucher:** gives a unique code for a percentage discount. It can be used in the storefront or Cashier POS, but never together with loyalty points.
- **Free product:** reserves one unit immediately. Cashier sees the claim and records the handover as a zero-value, auditable order.

## Create a hunt

1. Open **Master Control → POS Terminal**.
2. In **Hidden Treat Hunts**, choose the location, prize, dates, and winner limit.
3. For a storefront location, select the exact product card that will hide the gift.
4. Keep rewards small. A recommended first campaign is **1 point**, **5% off with a ₱100 minimum**, or a low-cost free item with **1–3 winners**.
5. Create the hunt. Use **Pause** at any time; existing claimed rewards remain valid until their shown expiry.

## Cashier steps

- For a voucher, select the member in Cashier POS and enter their `HUNT-...` code. The system checks ownership, expiry, minimum purchase, and one-time use before recording the sale.
- For a free product, use the **Hidden Treat free-product claims** panel in Cashier POS after physically handing over the prize.
- For a flexible-price product, every newly entered specific amount becomes its own cart row. For example, the same product entered at ₱20 and ₱27 remains two separate lines in the receipt.

## Important safeguards

- A member can claim each hunt once only.
- Products are not identifiable as prizes until a member finds the gift.
- Voucher codes belong to the claiming account, not to whoever types the code.
- The prize stock is reserved at discovery, protecting the winner from later normal sales.
- Use an expiry date and a winner limit to control costs.
