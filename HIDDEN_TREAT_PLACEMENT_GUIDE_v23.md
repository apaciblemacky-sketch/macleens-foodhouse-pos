# Hidden Treat placement controls

Open **Master Control → POS Terminal**, then scroll to **Product Bundle Deals & Discounts** and the **Hidden Treat Hunts** card.

## Create a new hunt

1. Choose the broad area: a selected storefront product, the loyalty portal, or Community.
2. Choose **Exact Display Area**. The available choices automatically change for the selected broad area.
3. Choose a **Prize Display Size** from 24 to 180 pixels.
4. Optionally upload a JPG, PNG, or WEBP prize picture (maximum 2.5 MB). If no picture is uploaded, the normal gift-box icon is shown.
5. Choose the reward and schedule, then create the hunt.

## Change an existing hunt

In the **Display** column, open the hunt’s details. You can move it to another valid area, change the size, replace the picture, or check **Use gift box instead** to remove the custom image. This does not reset claims, reward limits, or expiry dates.

Existing hunts remain safe: if they were created before this update, they automatically display in the normal default area for their broad location until you choose a new exact area.

Rewards are still claimable only once per signed-in loyalty account. Moving or resizing a prize never creates another claim.
