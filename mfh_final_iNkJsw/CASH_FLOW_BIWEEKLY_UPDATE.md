# Cash Flow Bi-weekly Update

The Cash Flow portal now supports **Bi-weekly (Every 2 Weeks)** schedules for both expenses and additional income.

- Bi-weekly means once every 14 days.
- Duration is the number of occurrences.
- Finite duration can now reach 540 bi-weekly occurrences, and Bi-weekly can also be set to Indefinite / No End Date.
- Existing Daily, Weekly, and Monthly schedules are unchanged.
