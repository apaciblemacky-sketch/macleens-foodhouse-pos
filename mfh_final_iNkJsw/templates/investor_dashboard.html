Investor-readiness checklist

Prepare these before moving a serious conversation toward an agreement.

  • 1
    Exact funding request. Use supplier quotations and a line-item budget—not a rough total.
  • 2
    12-month operating history. Reconcile sales, COGS, expenses, owner withdrawals, GCash, and bank records.
  • 3
    Unit economics. Show price, direct cost, gross margin, sales volume, spoilage, and labor assumptions by product line.
  • 4
    Milestones. Define what is purchased, when it launches, and which customer or revenue measure will prove progress.
  • 5
    Ownership and controls. State who manages funds, approvals, reporting, inventory, and cash reconciliation.
  • 6
    Risk and downside plan. Explain seasonality, slow-selling products, equipment delays, competition, and online acquisition cost.
  • 7
    Professional review. Have the final structure, disclosures, permits, taxes, and written agreement reviewed locally before accepting funds.

Suggested reporting: monthly profit-and-loss summary, cash reconciliation, inventory count, use-of-funds update, and milestone progress.
